## Overview

This project, hosted at the Korea Institute for Advanced Study (KIAS) Center for AI and Natural Sciences (CAINS), investigates generative model frameworks that are privacy-preserving, robust, and practically deployable.

## Objectives

1. **Private Generation** — Develop diffusion-based synthetic data pipelines that satisfy formal differential privacy guarantees while maintaining high generation quality.

2. **Machine Unlearning** — Design efficient algorithms to selectively remove data or concepts from pretrained generative models, addressing both copyright and privacy concerns.

3. **Fairness & Safety** — Ensure that generative models do not amplify demographic biases and can be steered toward safe outputs.

## Highlights

- Developed the CARE (Co-occurring Associated REtained concepts) framework for diffusion unlearning, accepted at ICLR 2026.
- Proposed DPTrainer, a method using in-distribution public data synthesis for DP-SGD training, accepted at CVPR 2024.
- Organized the KIAS CAINS Winter Workshop (Jan 2026) and hosted 10+ seminar speakers on generative AI and trustworthy ML.
