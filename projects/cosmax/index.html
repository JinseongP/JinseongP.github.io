Prediction and Generative AI for Color Cosmetics

Industry collaboration with COSMAX, the world's largest cosmetics OEM/ODM company, developing generative neural networks for color prediction and product formulation optimization.

Period Dec 2023 – Sep 2024
Role Lead Researcher
Partner COSMAX (코스맥스)

Overview

This industry collaboration with COSMAX — the world’s largest cosmetics OEM/ODM manufacturer — applied machine learning and generative AI to automate and improve color cosmetics product development.

Objectives

  1. Color Prediction — Develop neural network models that predict the final color appearance of cosmetic formulations (lipstick, foundation, eyeshadow) from ingredient compositions.

  2. Generative Formulation — Use generative models to propose novel ingredient combinations that achieve target color properties, reducing the need for costly manual iteration.

  3. Quality Assurance — Build anomaly detection pipelines for manufacturing quality control under limited and imbalanced production data.

Approach

We leveraged both supervised regression models and generative approaches (diffusion-based) to map between the high-dimensional ingredient space and perceptual color space. Privacy-aware data handling was a key design constraint given the proprietary nature of formulation data.

Impact

The collaboration accelerated the product development cycle for new color cosmetics lines and demonstrated that privacy-preserving generative AI can deliver practical value in a real manufacturing context.
