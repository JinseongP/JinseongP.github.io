About

I am an AI research fellow at Korea Institute for Advanced Study (KIAS) as a part of my alternative military service as a technical research personnel.
Currently, I am seeking collaborations in the fields of AI safety and time series analysis.
I will visit UBC (Vancouver, Canada) as a visiting Postdoc from June to early of August 2025.

B.S. (IME, POSTECH) → M.S./Ph.D. (SCLF Lab advised by Prof. Jaewook Lee, IE, SNU) → Postdoc (CAINS advised by Prof. Jeong Han Kim, KIAS) → Visiting Postdoc (Hosted by Prof. Mijung Park, UBC)

Topics of interest:

  • SAFE AI
    • (i) Generative diffusion models under limited data circumstances, such as privacy (CVPR'24) and fairness (AAAI'24).
    • (ii) Privacy-preserving methods to mitigate privacy-utility trade-offs (ICML'23, Information Sciences).
    • (iii) Defense methods against various attacks to address privacy and safety issues in AI model attacks (NeurIPS'21, NeurIPS'23, Pattern Recognition).
  • Time series analysis
    • (i) Synthetic time series data (arXiv) and its real-world applications, such as in finance (Comp' Econ').
    • (ii) Time series forecasting (NeurIPS'24) and generalization to mitigate the performance gap between training and test accuracies (Applied Soft Computing, ICML'23).
