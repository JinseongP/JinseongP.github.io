About

I am a 1st year Ph.D. student at Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST), advised by Prof. Jinwoo Shin. Prior to this, I received M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at KAIST and B.S. in Industrial Engineering and Mathematical Sciences (minor) at Seoul National University (SNU). My research interests focus on developing algorithms for specific data types, specifically (i) tabular data and (ii) molecular data. More recently, I am interested in utilizing foundation models (e.g., large language models, text-to-molecule models) to learn better representations.
