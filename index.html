Bio

I am a 3rd year Ph.D. student at at Seoul National University (SNU), advised by Prof. Jaewook Lee in Statistical Learning & Computational Finance (SLCF) Lab. I am also fulfilling my military service as part of my Ph.D. program. Prior to this, I received M.S. in Industrial Engineering at SNU and B.S. in Industrial and Management Engineering at Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH).

My research interests focus on developing algorithms for improving the generalization performance of deep learning with privacy (e.g., differential privacy) and security (e.g., adversarial attack). More recently, I am interested in discovering well-generalizing optimization (e.g., sharpness and flatness) and utilizing generative models (e.g., diffusion models) for data amplification. I'm always seeking new research collaborations and valuable opportunities including internship. Feel free to contact me at the email address provided above.
