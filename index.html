About

I am a 3rd year Ph.D. student at at Seoul National University (SNU), advised by Prof. Jaewook Lee in Statistical Learning & Computational Finance (SLCF) Lab. I am also fulfilling my military service as part of my Ph.D. program. Prior to this, I received M.S. in Industrial Engineering at SNU and B.S. in Industrial and Management Engineering at Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH).

Topics of interest (publications with overlap):

  • Privacy: Differentially private learning (ICML'23, Information Sciences) and data synthesis for privacy (CVPR'24, AAAI'24).
  • Time series: Deep learning models for time series (Applied Soft Computing) and financial applications.
  • Optimization: Discovering well-generalizing optimization (ICML'23, ICML'23, Applied Soft Computing) and security problems concerning adversarial attack (NeurIPS'21, NeurIPS'23, Pattern Recognition).
I'm always seeking new research collaborations and valuable opportunities including internship. Feel free to contact me at the email address provided above.
