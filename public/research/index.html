Research Overview

Ψ
PSI
Private
& Safe
Intelligence
We investigate the theory and algorithms of trustworthy machine learning. Our research spans three pillars: theoretical foundations of generative models and optimization; privacy & safety via differential privacy, machine unlearning, and adversarial robustness; and industrial applications in time series, manufacturing, and finance. Our mission is to build AI that is simultaneously private, safe, and intelligent.
1 🧠

Understanding of AI

We study the theoretical foundations of modern AI — how generative models learn distributions, why certain minima generalize better than others, and how to rigorously characterize the generalization gap. Our goal is to build principled understanding that informs better algorithm design.

🎨

Generative Modeling

Developing diffusion bridges and flow-based models for controllable generation, with applications to time series and image synthesis.

📊

Generalization Theory

Analyzing loss landscape geometry and sharpness measures to understand when and why deep networks generalize.

⚡

Optimization

Designing and analyzing adaptive optimizers (SAM, DP-SAM) and studying their implicit regularization effects.

2 🔒

Privacy & Safety Issues of AI

We develop rigorous methods to ensure that AI systems protect individual privacy and are robust against adversarial interference. Our work spans formal privacy guarantees via differential privacy, machine unlearning for generative AI, and defenses against adversarial attacks.

🛡️

Differential Privacy

Optimizing the utility-privacy tradeoff for ML models trained with DP-SGD, including private SVMs and synthetic data generation.

🗑️

Machine Unlearning

Efficiently removing specific data or concepts from trained models (including diffusion models) without full retraining.

⚔️

Adversarial Robustness

Understanding certified robustness, transferable adversarial examples in speech, and membership inference in RAG systems.

3 📈

Industrial Applications

We apply trustworthy AI techniques to real-world industrial challenges. Our collaborations with industry partners span manufacturing quality prediction, financial time series modeling, and anomaly detection under data scarcity — always with safety and privacy in mind.

📉

Time Series

Robust forecasting and generation for noisy, scarce sequential data; self-attention analysis for time series transformers.

💹

Finance

Asset price modeling with generative diffusion models, portfolio insurance strategies, and volatility forecasting with XAI.

🏭

Manufacturing

Privacy-preserving anomaly detection for imbalanced industrial data; generative AI for cosmetics color prediction (COSMAX).
